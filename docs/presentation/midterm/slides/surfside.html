SECOND LOOK · FRAME 02 / 12

(45 s) Surfside, Miami. [The animation runs full screen.] A residential tower, its garage under a flat pool deck. The 2018 report: the waterproofing under the deck had failed and water was getting into the slab. In the garage, abundant cracking and spalling in the columns, the beams, the walls. The cracks were photographed. Three years passed. On the night of 24 June 2021 the building partly collapsed: 98 people died. The cause is still under federal investigation. But the cracks were already in the photos: documented in 2018, collapsed in 2021.
