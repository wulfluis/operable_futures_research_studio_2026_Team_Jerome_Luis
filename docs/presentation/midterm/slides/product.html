(40 s) What does Second Look do? A resident sends one phone photo and gets one of five answers. Retake if the photo is unusable. Expert needed if the model is unsure: doubt never becomes Category 1. Then three categories, from routine monitoring to "an engineer must confirm". Every answer has a reason and a confidence. And it never says "safe": it is triage, not a clearance.
