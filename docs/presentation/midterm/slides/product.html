SECOND LOOK · FRAME 05 / 12

(40 s) What does Second Look do? First: we only look at cracks in concrete. A resident sends one phone photo, and it goes through two checks. Is the photo usable? If not: Retake. Is the model sure? If not: Expert needed, because doubt never becomes Category 1. If both pass, the risk matrix gives one of three categories: monitor, repair and recheck, or an engineer must confirm. Every answer has a one-sentence reason. And it never says "safe": it is triage, not a clearance.
