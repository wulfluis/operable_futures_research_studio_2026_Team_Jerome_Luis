SECOND LOOK · FRAME 03 / 12

(40 s) Rana Plaza, near Dhaka, 2013. [The animation runs full screen.] Eight storeys: shops and a bank below, garment factories above. It was built to fail: the eastern half stands on a filled-in pond, the upper four floors were added without a permit, and heavy generators sit on top. On 23 April cracks appeared; a local TV crew filmed them and the building was evacuated. The owner declared it safe, and the workers were told to come back the next day. On the morning of 24 April it collapsed: 1,134 people died and about 2,500 were injured. The cracks were seen the day before.
