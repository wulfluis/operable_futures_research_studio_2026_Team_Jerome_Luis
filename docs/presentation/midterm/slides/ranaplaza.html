RANA PLAZA, SAVAR, DHAKA

23 Apr 2013 · Cracks appear in an eight-storey building of garment factories. A TV crew films them; it is evacuated.

24 Apr 2013 · Declared safe, workers are sent back in. It collapses that morning.

1,134 dead

(40 s) Rana Plaza, near Dhaka, 2013. [The animation runs on the right.] Eight storeys: shops and a bank below, garment factories above. On 23 April cracks appeared; a local TV crew filmed them and the building was evacuated. The owner declared it safe, and the workers were told to come back the next day. On the morning of 24 April it collapsed: 1,134 people died and about 2,500 were injured. The cracks were seen the day before.
