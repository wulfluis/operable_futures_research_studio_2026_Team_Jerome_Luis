[The dots: each one is one per cent of humanity.] UN-Habitat counts more than 3 billion people in inadequate housing, 1.16 billion of them in slums and informal settlements, 270 million more slum dwellers than in 2000. Ageing concrete at the end of its life cycle. The people affected see the cracks every day, but cannot tell a harmless one from a dangerous one, and engineers are too few to visit every building.
