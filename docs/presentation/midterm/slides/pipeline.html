(50 s) Under the hood it is a computer vision pipeline in six steps. One: a quality gate checks sharpness with two blur measures; a bad photo gets Retake. Two: a U-Net segments every crack pixel. Three and four, next: width in millimetres from a printed scale card, and the crack pattern. Five: written, auditable rules give the level and the reason. Six: an engineer confirms every Category 3. The AI finds and measures; rules decide; a human confirms.
