SECOND LOOK · FRAME 11 / 12

(40 s) Where next. At the hackathon: width in millimetres from a printed scale card, and the close-up check. On 3 December: our own photos, rated by engineers, so we can compare the AI against an engineer's visual check. Final on 17 December. And one piece we have not started yet: a short survey that goes with every photo. We only look at cracks in concrete, and how severe a crack is depends on where it is: three scales: the material, concrete only; the element, a column, a beam, a slab or a wall; and the building: which storey, how old it is, and how it has been maintained. We will design these questions together with structural engineers, because they know what matters.
