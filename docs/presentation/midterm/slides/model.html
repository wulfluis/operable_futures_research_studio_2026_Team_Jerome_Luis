(45 s) Does it work? We trained a U-Net with a ResNet-34 encoder on 9,441 public crack photos, plus brick walls without cracks: about one hour on a laptop GPU. On 1,203 test photos it had never seen, it found 538 of 550 cracks and raised 7 false alarms on 653 clean walls. The 12 it missed are mostly faint hairlines on painted plaster. The real test is on 3 December: the same photos against an engineer's visual check, our non-AI baseline.
