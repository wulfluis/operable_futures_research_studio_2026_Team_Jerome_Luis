(45 s) Does it work? [Each square is one test photo.] The bar at the bottom is the data. 8,241 photos to learn from: 8,082 public crack close-ups from CrackSeg9k and crack-seg, plus 159 brick walls without cracks so it stops reading mortar joints as cracks. 660 photos only to pick the best of 25 rounds, never to learn from. The model is a U-Net whose ResNet-34 part was pre-trained on ImageNet and then fine-tuned on cracks, on our laptop GPU. And 1,203 test photos it had never seen, run once at the end: these are the squares. On them it found 538 of 550 cracks and raised 7 false alarms on 653 clean walls. We still have to look at the 12 it missed; in the first version the misses were mostly faint hairlines on painted plaster. The real test is on 3 December: the same photos against an engineer's visual check, our non-AI baseline.
