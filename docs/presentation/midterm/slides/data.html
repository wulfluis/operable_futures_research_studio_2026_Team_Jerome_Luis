What data exists, and what doesn’t.

TRAIN · PUBLIC

Crack pixels

9,441 photos with crack masks (CrackSeg9k, crack-seg), plus walls without cracks (METU, SDNET2018, Darbhanga brick).

TEST · CLOSEST TO REALITY

Damaged buildings

Engineer surveys after Haiti 2010 and Nepal 2015: light, moderate, severe. One rating per building, no scale.

MISSING · WE COLLECT

Rated cracks in mm

Phone photos with a scale card, each crack rated by an engineer. No public dataset has this.

AI can learn to find crack pixels. It cannot invent an engineer’s rating, so we don’t.

(45 s) What data exists? Enough public data to teach a model to find crack pixels: 9,441 photos with crack masks, plus walls without cracks. Closest to reality: engineer surveys after the Haiti and Nepal earthquakes, but one rating per building and no scale. What is missing is exactly what triage needs: a crack measured in millimetres and rated by an engineer. No public dataset has it, so we collect it ourselves with a printed scale card. AI can find crack pixels; it cannot invent an engineer's rating, and we don't let it.
