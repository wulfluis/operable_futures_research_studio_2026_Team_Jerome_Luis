# Questions and the knock (D120)
- After photo 1, one question at a time, each with "I don't know". 12 questions in `src/app/questions.py`.
- Where the crack sits (side, floor, floors above, element, window or door corner, junction): context and training data.
- Signs the matrix knows: sticking doors, earthquake, added floors, damp, sagging. A "yes" sets the sign (raises only). "Changed since": context; the growth sign needs two measured photos.
- Answers are kept with the result and on the wall for the engineer.
- Knock: a recording next to the crack; pitch (Hz), ringing (ms to −20 dB), number of knocks. The sound is not kept. No hollow or solid reading until engineers label knocks.
- "How sure is the app today": measured numbers only.
