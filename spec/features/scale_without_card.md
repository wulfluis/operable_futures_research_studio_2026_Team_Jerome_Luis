# Scale without a card (D116)
- Only mm are shown. Pixels stay inside the app.
- Bank card: measured. Shown with its error.
- No card: a door seen whole and upright in photo 2 (SAM 3.1, score ≥ 0.5, height/width 1.6 to 3.6, not cut by the photo's edge) gives mm per pixel of photo 2; the link of the two photos (D66, D75) carries it to photo 1 at the crack.
- Door 2.10 m and error ± 16 %: assumptions, illustrative until compared with card photos.
- Every estimated width is "≈" with its error (scale share + one pixel). An estimated width never gives Category 1 (Expert needed).
- No scale: no width number; colours relative to the crack only.
- Not used: people (rule 9), scaffolding (no standard size found).
- Code: `src/measure/photo2_scale.py`, `core.estimate_scale`. Tests: `tests/test_review_26nov.py`, `tests/test_crack_view.py`.
